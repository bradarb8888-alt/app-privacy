QR Snap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

QR Snap is published by Free the People Apps. Contact: bradarb8888@gmail.com.

What the app does

QR Snap scans and generates QR codes and barcodes on your device. Scan history is stored only on your phone.

Data we collect

We do not require an account. Camera access is used only to scan codes when you choose. We do not upload your scans to our servers.

Advertising

The free app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may use an advertising ID and device info for third-party advertising. See Google Privacy Policy.

Children

QR Snap is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.

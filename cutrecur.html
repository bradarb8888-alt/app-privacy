CutRecur

The honest subscription tracker. 100% free. No account. No bank link. No ads.

What it does

  • Add each recurring charge once: name, amount (CAD by default), weekly / monthly / yearly, next charge date, notes, cancel link.
  • See your real monthly total (weekly × 4.33, yearly ÷ 12) and yearly cost.
  • Get a reminder N days before each charge (default 3) — then tap Keep or Mark cancelled.
  • Cancelled subscriptions stay in history and show what you're saving per month.
  • Export everything to CSV through the share sheet.

Everything is free for everyone — no Pro tier, no in-app purchases, no feature limits.

Download

Android APK (sideload): published on the releases page when available. Enable “Install unknown apps” for your browser, then open the APK. Requires Android 8.0+.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Publisher: Direct Apps.

CutRecur has no account, no bank connection, no ads, no analytics, and does not request the internet permission. The subscriptions you enter are stored only in a database on your device. We never receive, sell, or share them.

Notifications: if you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
Export: a CSV file is created only when you tap Export and is shared only with the app you choose.
Deletion: delete entries in the app, or uninstall to remove all data.
Children: not directed at children; no personal data is collected.

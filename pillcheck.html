PillCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Publisher: Direct Apps
Contact: bradarb8888@gmail.com

Summary

PillCheck works entirely offline. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. The information you enter never leaves your device, and we (Direct Apps) cannot see it.

Information you enter

PillCheck stores the following only in a private database on your device:

  • Medication names, optional dose notes, and the times you set for reminders.
  • A log of which doses you marked as taken, and when.
  • Your settings (for example, whether reminders are on).

This information may be health-related. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show your dose reminders. You can deny or turn this off at any time in the app's Settings or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup, wake lock, network state, foreground service: added automatically by Android's WorkManager library so reminders survive restarts and run efficiently. PillCheck never connects to the internet.
  • PillCheck does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, storage, or exact-alarm permissions.

Third parties

PillCheck contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include PillCheck's data in your own Google backup under your account and Google's terms. Direct Apps has no access to these backups.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove a medication in the app, clear all data in Android Settings > Apps > PillCheck > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app, which deletes everything.

Children

PillCheck is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

PillCheck is a reminder and log tool. It does not give medical advice. Reminders may be delayed by Android's battery saving. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, note them in the app's release notes.

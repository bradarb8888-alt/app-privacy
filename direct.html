Direct privacy policy

Direct is a QR and barcode scanner. Scans, notes, and generated codes stay on the device. The app does not create an account, does not sell data, and does not send scan contents to a server.

The camera is used only to read a code or a picture you pick. Shared images are scanned locally. History is stored in app storage and can be deleted in the app.

Contact: use the support email on the store listing.

Obscura — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Obscura is made by Free the People Apps. Contact: bradarb8888@gmail.com.

What we collect: Nothing. Obscura has no internet permission, no ads, and no analytics. Photos, videos, documents, PINs, and encryption keys stay on your device only.

How it works: Files you hide are encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM. Your PIN or passphrase unlocks a vault key sealed with the Android Keystore. We cannot read your vault and there is no account or cloud backup.

Permissions: Only what Android needs for the system file picker and local storage. Obscura does not access the network.

Children: Obscura is for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes: If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
